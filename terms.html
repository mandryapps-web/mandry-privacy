New places and photos go through manual review before other users see them. We may approve, reject or edit a submission (for example, to fix coordinates or merge a duplicate). We may reject a submission without detailed reasons, but we'll show you its status in the app. While a photo or suggestion is pending or rejected, you can delete it yourself.

A person reviews reports. Moderation in Mandry is done manually by the operator. A report is not proof that content breaks the rules: we look at the content before deciding, and we don't take measures only because something was reported.

What we can do. After reviewing, we may remove a comment (a comment that has replies is replaced by a “deleted” placeholder without text or author, so the conversation stays readable), remove a photo or place image, hide a place's card, reset a nickname or remove an avatar, or reject a submission. Hiding a place hides its card from other users (people who saved it see “Place unavailable”); it does not delete other users' saved places, reviews or trips. We may also restrict features or close an account (see “Suspension & termination”).

Notices. If we act on your content, we tell you, in the app where available: what we did, whether the basis was these rules or the law, the type of reason, a short note where we choose to add one, and a decision number. For the most serious cases (for example child safety, or where we are restricted from describing the content) the notice is general. We don't tell you who reported your content.

Files. When we remove a photo, image or avatar, we move the file from its public address to private storage that only we can access. This is a separate step carried out after the decision: it can take time, can fail and be retried, and we can't promise that the file is unreachable until we have confirmed that it was moved. Copies already cached by a browser, a content-delivery network (CDN) or another service, or saved by other people, are outside our control. We don't delete moderated files automatically. Where the law requires, we may keep moderated material or hand it to the competent authorities.

Coins. Moderating content doesn't automatically take back coins that were already earned — see “Coins & rewards”.

We may remove content that is already published — including comments and reviews — if it breaks these Terms, infringes someone's rights, or has to be removed for legal reasons.

You can ask us to remove an approved photo or place — see “Reports, blocking & appeals” below. Deleting your account itself is available at any time from Profile → Delete account; the Privacy Policy lists what is removed and what is not (for example, image files of photos that were never approved are not deleted automatically, and copies held by service providers stay for their own retention periods).

Comments. If you delete a comment, or delete your account, a comment that has replies is replaced by a “deleted” placeholder without text or author so that replies stay readable; a comment without replies is removed. Your reports, and the records of moderation decisions about your content, are described in the Privacy Policy — they stay after you delete your account without a working link to your profile, but they may still contain information that identifies you (see the Privacy Policy).
