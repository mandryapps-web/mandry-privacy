Privacy Policy

What Mandry collects when you plan a trip, why we collect it, and how to see or remove it.

In short: Mandry stores what you give it — your login, the places you save and suggest, the photos you attach — to run the app and its moderation queue. It does not track your location in the background, does not run ad or analytics trackers, and never sees your card number: purchases go through Google Play's own billing.

01 What we collect

Everything below is information you give us directly, by using the app.

Account

Your email address, and either a password (stored by our database provider, Supabase, using standard salted hashing — we never see it in plain text) or, if you choose "Continue with Google," the name, email, and profile photo Google shares with us for sign-in.

Profile

Nickname, avatar photo, your chosen app language and light/dark/pink/gradient theme, and — if you set it — your home country, used only to show a "you're travelling" prompt.

Content you create

  • Places you save, mark as visited, or write a comment or recommendation on
  • New places you suggest, including any photos and the Google Maps link you attach
  • Photos you contribute to existing places
  • Messages you send through the feedback form

Purchases

When you buy coins, Google Play Billing (via RevenueCat) tells us which pack you bought and confirms payment — we record that as a coin transaction. We never receive or store your card details; Google and RevenueCat handle the transaction itself.

What we don't currently collect: precise or background location, and push-notification tokens. Both are built into the app's code but switched off while Mandry is in this phase of development. If either is turned on in a future update, we'll ask for your permission first and update this policy.

02 How we use it

  • Run your account and sync your saved places, coins, and progress across devices
  • Review place and photo suggestions before they go live — see "Who we share it with" below
  • Send account emails: sign-in links, password resets, purchase confirmations
  • Reply to feedback you send us

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell it.

04 How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account (see below), it's removed along with almost everything tied to it.

One exception: if a place you suggested was already approved and published, it stays in the app the way a published wiki edit would — it's no longer linked to your account or name once you delete it.

05 Your controls

From Profile → Delete account inside the app, you can permanently delete your account. That immediately removes your profile, saved places, reviews and comments, place and photo suggestions, feedback history, and coin transaction history — in one step, no support ticket needed.

You can also change your nickname, avatar, language, and theme at any time from the same Profile screen.

06 Security

Your data lives on Supabase's infrastructure with row-level access controls, meaning the app itself only ever asks for your own data, not anyone else's. Passwords are hashed, never stored as plain text, and all traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted.

07 Children

Mandry isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

08 Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app.

09 Contact

Questions about this policy or your data — write to mandryapps@gmail.com.
