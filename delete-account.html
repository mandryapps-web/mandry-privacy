Delete your Mandry account

How to delete your MandryApp account and data — in the app, or by email if you no longer have the app.

In short: You can delete your MandryApp account yourself in the app (Profile → Delete account). If you don't have the app or can't sign in, email us from your account's address and we'll handle the request within 30 days. Approved photos and places stay in the shared catalog without your name, and some copies kept by other services remain for a while — the details are below.

01 Delete in the app

  1. Open Mandry and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and choose Delete account.
  3. Follow the on-screen instructions and confirm the deletion.

Deletion starts immediately and can't be undone. If an error appears, check your connection and try again, or use the email route below.

02 Delete by email — without the app

If the app isn't installed, or you can't sign in, you can ask us to delete your account without opening the app:

  1. Email mandryapps@gmail.com from the address linked to your Mandry account.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my Mandry account" and, if you can, mention the nickname you used.

We'll remove the data described below (subject to the exceptions listed under "What stays") within 30 days and confirm by email once it's done.

Confirming it's your account

If your request comes from an address that isn't linked to the account, or we aren't sure it comes from the account holder, we may ask you for additional confirmation of ownership — for example to write to us from the account address, or to confirm details of the account. We will never ask for your password. If we can't confirm ownership, we may not be able to delete the account.

03 What is deleted

Deleting your account removes:

  • your profile, including your avatar image, and your sign-in account (your email and login)
  • your saved places, reviews and comments
  • your trip plans
  • place and photo suggestions that were not approved
  • your feedback history
  • your coin balance and transaction history
  • the push-notification tokens linked to your account

Your analytics choice, which is stored only on your device, is removed when you delete your account in the app. The app removes your avatar image file from storage when you delete your account; if that step fails, email us and we'll remove it. The Privacy Policy describes all of this in more detail.

04 What stays

Some things are not removed, or are removed later:

  • Approved places and photos you contributed stay in Mandry's shared catalog. They are shown without your name and are no longer linked to your profile in our database. To ask us to remove one, see below.
  • Image files of photos that were not approved are not deleted automatically with your account. They may stay online at their web addresses until we remove them; email us to have them removed.
  • Copies of suggestions and feedback messages already sent to our private moderation channel on Telegram.
  • Purchase records held by Google and RevenueCat under their own terms.
  • Crash, error and analytics data held by Google (Firebase) and Sentry stays until their retention periods end — Google Analytics events 2 months and user-level data 14 months, Crashlytics reports up to about 90 days according to Google, Sentry reports 30 days on our current plan.

Data held by our service providers. If you want to request deletion of personal data held by our service providers, contact us at mandryapps@gmail.com. We will review your request and take the steps required by applicable data protection law.

Removing an approved photo or place. Email mandryapps@gmail.com with the place or photo you mean (a link or the place name and city) and why. We review each request individually, taking applicable law into account.

05 Coins

Coins belong to your account. If you delete your account, any unused coins — including coins you bought — are lost. This doesn't affect any rights you have by law as a consumer.
