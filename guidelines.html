01 Why these rules

Mandry is a shared travel catalog: what you add — comments, photos, places, reviews — is seen by other travellers. These rules protect them and you. They are part of the Terms of Service, and both share one rules version (2026-11-24). When you create an account or sign in, you agree to them (see “Acceptance” in the Terms). The Privacy Policy describes how we handle data.

02 The short version

  • Be honest. Real places, real visits, accurate information.
  • Be respectful. Disagree without insulting, threatening or targeting anyone.
  • Stay safe. Don't share anything that puts people at risk — including bad advice about access or safety.
  • Share only what's yours. Your photos, your words, or content you have the right to use.
  • Don't play the system. No duplicate accounts, empty comments or copy-paste submissions to earn coins.

03 What is never allowed

You may not post, upload or share content that:

  • Is illegal, or helps someone break the law.
  • Involves violence, threats, harassment or bullying, or encourages harm to people or animals.
  • Is hateful or discriminatory — attacks people because of race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, sexual orientation, disability or similar traits.
  • Is sexually explicit, or involves minors in any sexual way. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material and treat it as the most serious case.
  • Exposes other people's private life or personal data — home addresses, phone numbers, documents, or photos in which a person is identifiable and hasn't agreed to be shown.
  • Infringes copyright or other rights — photos or text you don't have the right to use, or someone else's brand used to mislead.
  • Is spam, advertising or a scam, links to doubtful or harmful sites, or malware.
  • Is deliberately false or dangerous — for example, wrong information about a place's safety, access or conditions that could lead someone into harm.
  • Impersonates another person, or misrepresents your connection to a place.

04 Rules by type of content

Comments and replies

Keep them about the place, polite and useful. No advertising, repeated or copy-pasted text, or personal data of other people. Replies are one level deep.

Reviews, visits and recommendations

Mark a place as visited, or recommend it, only if you've been there. Don't try to inflate or lower a place's standing.

Photos

Upload only photos you took or have the right to share. No screenshots, other people's photos or stock images; no photos showing people who haven't agreed; no sexually explicit images. Photos are reviewed before other users see them, and approved photos are stored at public web addresses (see the Privacy Policy).

Places you suggest

Suggest real places that exist and are open to the public, with accurate coordinates. No private property, restricted or dangerous areas, duplicates of places already in Mandry, or advertising. Suggestions are reviewed before they appear.

Nicknames and avatars

No insults, other people's names or brands used to deceive, personal data, or explicit images. We may reset a nickname or remove an avatar that breaks these rules.

Feedback messages

Messages you send us through the feedback form are private to us, but the same rules apply: no threats, harassment or illegal content.

05 Coins & rewards

Coins reward genuine contributions, such as a place or photo we approve after review or a first comment on a place. Don't earn them with duplicates, empty or copy-pasted comments, scripts, bots or extra accounts. Rewards are described in the Terms. If we remove content for breaking these rules, we don't automatically take back coins you already earned, and content we restore after an appeal doesn't earn a reward a second time. Deleting your account, however, deletes your coin balance, including coins you earned.

06 Reporting & blocking

Some of these tools — reporting content in the app, blocking users, in-app notices about moderation decisions and in-app appeals — are being added to Mandry through app updates, and they apply from the app version that includes them. Until your version has them, and at any time, you can write to mandryapps@gmail.com.

Report. In app versions that include it, use “Report” on a comment, a photo added to a place, a place suggested by a user, an image that a user uploaded to a place card, a nickname or an avatar. Other kinds of content (for example reviews) can't be reported in the app — write to us. Choose “Breaks the rules” or “Potentially illegal”, pick a reason and, if you wish, add a short description (for potentially illegal content, you confirm your report is accurate to the best of your knowledge). The Report action identifies the item for us, so you don't need to copy or link anything. If the material is harmful, don't copy, download, forward or attach it, and don't paste links to it in the description — just describe where it is. For the most serious material (for example sexual content involving minors), never send it to us or to anyone else. By email, describe where it is in Mandry (place name and city, nickname, date) and why.

Being reported doesn't mean content breaks the rules: a person looks at it first, and we don't take measures only because it was reported. We don't tell the author who reported. To prevent abuse we may limit how many rule-breaking reports one person can send in a short period; we always save reports about potentially illegal content. Please don't misuse reports.

Block. In app versions that include it, you can block a user. Their comments are hidden from you, and neither of you can reply directly to the other's comments. Blocking doesn't remove their content for others, and they aren't told. Manage blocks in Profile → Blocked users.

07 What we do

A person reviews each report and decides. Depending on what we find, we may remove or hide a comment, photo or place image, hide a place's card, reset a nickname, remove an avatar, reject a submission, restrict features, or — for serious or repeated violations — close an account. We tell you (in the app where available) what we did, whether the basis was these rules or the law, and the type of reason; for the most serious cases the notice is general. We tell the person who reported (in the app where available) what the outcome was (for example, that we found a violation and acted, or found none), without details about the author. Moderated files are moved to private storage in a separate step that can take time or be retried, so a file may stay reachable at its old address, or in caches and copies outside our control, until the move is confirmed; if a decision is cancelled, the file may be moved back. Moderated files are not deleted automatically. Where the law requires, we may keep moderated material or hand it to the competent authorities.

08 Appeals

If you disagree with a decision about your content or about your report, you can appeal it once (in app versions that include it, from the decision notice in Notifications, with up to 1,000 characters of explanation), or write to mandryapps@gmail.com with the decision number. A person reviews the appeal. If it's accepted, we cancel the decision and, where possible, restore the content — which isn't possible if the content was changed or deleted, another restriction applies, you deleted your account, or in the most serious cases. We tell you the result and whether the content was restored. More in the Terms.

09 Changes to these Guidelines

We may update these Guidelines. The date and rules version at the top of this page change, and for significant changes we'll notify you in the app and, if they need your agreement, ask you to agree again before you can post (see “Changes to these Terms” in the Terms).
